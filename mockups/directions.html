Three ways Fluidity could feel

The same three popups (launcher, emoji, power) in three directions, all in Fluid colours. None of them are boxes with monospace text. Pick one, mix them ("A's pill + B's type"), or reject all three.

A

Liquid

Fits the name. The bar is a small floating pill, and every popup grows out of it like water: rounded, glowing, the selection a lime pill that slides. Motion is the main idea: it stretches open, and goes back into the bar when it closes.

Editorial

No panels at all. The screen goes dark and your words show up big in a serif, like a magazine. Calm, very personal, and nothing like any other rice. Text-first, keyboard-first: numbers jump straight to a line.

Depth

Everything is a floating card with real depth. Results are separate cards tilted slightly away from you, and the selected one lifts toward you and glows. Playful and physical, like objects on a desk.
